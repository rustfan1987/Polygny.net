# Project Vision

## Status

This document defines the established project vision for Polygyny.net.

It covers only the project purpose, intended audience, problems the platform exists to solve, and long-term direction established in planning.

Items not yet settled are marked as unresolved rather than inferred.

## Purpose

Polygyny.net is intended to become a modern, multilingual platform centered on polygyny, family formation, practical household knowledge, community, education, publishing, and relationship discovery.

The project is intended to modernize a fragmented and often technically outdated ecosystem by giving users one place to learn, publish, search, connect, organize, and eventually use structured compatibility tools.

The platform is not intended to function only as a dating site.

It is intended to support the full lifecycle around family and community formation, including education, contributor publishing, reference material, practical guides, community organization, relationship discovery, and long-term participation after relationships are formed.

The platform is intended to be constructive, serious, practical, and institutionally stable rather than built around outrage, argument, or constant ideological conflict.

## Core Vision

The long-term vision is to create a central digital hub where people interested in polygyny, large families, interdependent households, self-sufficiency, practical family knowledge, and related community structures can participate in one coherent system.

The platform is intended to combine several currently fragmented functions:

- contributor publishing
- searchable articles and videos
- a structured wiki and citation system
- practical instructional material
- multilingual translation
- multilingual search
- contributor and community pages
- account-based following and personalized content
- structured questionnaires and quizzes
- compatibility-based relationship discovery
- private messaging and controlled profile disclosure
- verified membership
- translated interpersonal communication
- community organization
- future events, media, and commerce

These systems are intended to reinforce one another rather than exist as isolated features.

## Audience

The platform is intended for adults who are interested in, curious about, supportive of, participating in, researching, teaching about, or otherwise engaging constructively with polygyny and related family structures.

The audience is broader than one religious tradition.

Established planning explicitly rejects defining the platform as exclusively Biblical, Torah, Christian, Muslim, or otherwise denominational.

Biblical, Torah-oriented, religious, secular, culturally traditional, and other polygyny-positive users may participate as long as they follow the platform’s standards.

The platform is also intended to remain usable by people who are curious about polygyny but do not ultimately choose it.

The future matching system is intended to support users seeking monogamy as well as users seeking polygyny, with relationship-structure preferences treated as compatibility inputs rather than a condition of basic membership.

The intended audience also includes:

- existing plural families
- couples considering family expansion
- single users
- contributors and authors
- researchers and scholars
- wiki editors
- translators
- communities and organizations
- event organizers
- creators with existing YouTube, podcast, article, book, or social-media audiences
- people interested in practical household and self-sufficiency knowledge
- people interested in large-family economics, parenting, homesteading, household logistics, and community life

The platform is intended to be international rather than limited to the United States.

## Problems the Platform Exists to Solve

### Fragmented Knowledge

Relevant material currently exists across forums, personal websites, YouTube channels, podcasts, Telegram groups, Facebook groups, books, PDFs, old essays, scattered archives, and individual contributors.

The platform is intended to consolidate and organize that information so users can find it easily.

### Outdated Community Infrastructure

Existing communities often rely on traditional forums and scattered social-media platforms.

The project intends to replace the need to manually scan threads and disconnected sources with a modern feed, follows, alerts, search, filtering, weekly summaries, and account-aware content discovery.

### Poor Contributor Organization

Creators often have work spread across multiple platforms.

The platform is intended to give contributors a dedicated page where users can find their articles, videos, collaborations, external links, related communities, and other contributions in one place.

### Language Barriers

Most existing communities and content ecosystems are fragmented by language.

Polygyny.net is intended to make multilingual access a foundational capability rather than a later add-on.

The established initial language direction is English, Spanish, and Portuguese.

Content is intended to be translated, indexed, and presented across supported languages while preserving one canonical underlying content object.

Long-term language expansion is intended to be demand-driven and operationally manageable.

### Weak Search and Discoverability

The platform is intended to make articles, wiki material, videos, contributors, communities, and later other content types highly searchable.

Search is intended to work across supported languages and use structured indexing and enrichment rather than relying only on literal title matches.

### Lack of a Durable Reference Source

The wiki is intended to become a long-term reference system where historical, theological, cultural, practical, and other relevant knowledge can be organized, sourced, cited, revised, and linked.

Articles are intended to cite wiki pages.

Wiki pages are intended to cite articles and external sources.

The platform is intended to preserve the distinction between reference material, interpretation, opinion, and primary or secondary sources.

### Lack of Practical Family Infrastructure

The platform is intended to support more than theological or relationship discussion.

Practical material may include:

- homesteading
- food preparation
- large-family recipes
- household economics
- raising animals
- building and repair
- parenting
- logistics
- self-sufficiency
- practical manuals
- community knowledge

Marble Vault has been established conceptually as a practical instructional layer for guides and manuals.

Articles, wiki material, and Marble Vault content are intended to remain distinct in purpose.

### Poor Relationship Discovery

The future relationship system is intended to move away from swipe-based dating and unrestricted browsing.

The established direction is compatibility-based discovery using structured questionnaires, quizzes, profile sections, radar-style profile dimensions, and controlled disclosure.

Users are intended to discover one another based on meaningful compatibility rather than mass exposure.

### Privacy and Attention Protection

The future matching system is intended to protect user privacy and attention.

A user should not automatically become visible to every other user.

Compatibility thresholds, pseudonymity, selective profile disclosure, mutual reveal, and controlled messaging are intended to reduce harassment and mass unsolicited attention.

### Lack of Trust Signals

The platform is intended to support a paid Verified membership where a third-party identity process confirms who the member is.

Verification is intended to provide a stronger trust signal and stronger consequences for malicious behavior.

Verification is not intended to imply moral trustworthiness or guarantee behavior.

### Cross-Language Relationship Friction

The long-term platform is intended to allow users who speak different languages to communicate through translated chat and group conversations.

The original communication is intended to remain canonical, with translation acting as a derived representation.

This is intended to reduce language as a barrier to international relationships and community participation.

## Publishing Vision

The platform is intended to provide controlled, high-quality publishing rather than open posting by every registered user.

Authorship is intended to be an approved privilege.

Prospective authors are expected to request authorship and provide evidence of prior work or credibility.

Authorship can be revoked for violating platform rules.

The public-facing feed is intended to prioritize constructive, educational, practical, historical, theological, cultural, and family-oriented material.

The platform is not intended to center rage bait, personal attacks, call-out culture, hostile gender grievance content, or adversarial debate.

Contributor pages may contain a broader range of a creator’s work than the main public feed.

## Main Feed Vision

The homepage is intended to function as a modern, information-rich activity surface for the platform.

Established feed concepts include:

- uniform multimedia content cards
- exactly one expanded card at a time
- featured content
- For You
- Following
- Latest
- Featured
- Site News
- content-type filtering
- contributor following
- community following
- topic following
- language-aware presentation

The feed is intended to surface useful platform activity without becoming cluttered by every minor system event.

Questionnaire questions and similar low-level participation prompts are not intended to dominate the main content feed.

## Contributor Vision

Contributors are intended to receive meaningful value from participating.

A contributor page may organize:

- articles
- videos
- collaborations
- external channels
- books or projects
- related communities
- wiki contributions
- related events
- other published work

The platform is intended to amplify existing creators rather than require them to abandon their current channels.

A creator should be able to continue using YouTube, Patreon, podcasts, social media, websites, books, or other platforms while using Polygyny.net as an additional distribution, archival, translation, and discovery layer.

Long-term social-media operations may use contributor content, with permission, to create clips and translated promotional material.

## Wiki Vision

The wiki is intended to become a central reference archive.

Its purpose is to make difficult-to-find historical, theological, cultural, practical, and related information easier to locate and cite.

The wiki is intended to support:

- revision history
- source classification
- citations
- internal linking
- article references
- contributor references
- human review
- multilingual translation
- structured search
- long-term archival value

Accepted wiki contributions are intended to become part of the collaborative knowledge base rather than remain individually revocable authored articles.

The final legal and licensing structure for this has not yet been established.

## Marble Vault Vision

Marble Vault is intended to support practical, procedural knowledge.

Its established conceptual distinction is:

- articles explain, teach, argue, reflect, or present authored material
- wiki pages document reference knowledge
- Marble Vault guides explain how to do something

Potential guide topics include:

- recipes
- homesteading
- livestock
- household projects
- repairs
- gardening
- food preservation
- large-family logistics
- self-sufficiency
- practical family systems

Long-term plans may allow users to download guide collections as PDFs and potentially order custom printed books.

The exact integration boundaries between Marble Vault and Polygyny.net remain unresolved.

## Multilingual Vision

Multilingual operation is a foundational project goal.

The established initial language direction is:

- English
- Spanish
- Portuguese

The platform is intended to use locale-first URLs.

Examples conceptually follow the form:

`/en/...`

`/es/...`

`/pt/...`

A content item is intended to have one canonical identity and one original language.

Localized versions remain representations of the same underlying content rather than separate unrelated records.

New supported languages should be capable of being added later through a controlled backfill and translation workflow.

The platform is intended to track translation state, indexing state, revision relationships, stale translations, failures, and review status.

The long-term goal is for users in different languages to participate in the same content and community ecosystem rather than separate language silos.

## Search Vision

Search is intended to be universal across the platform while still distinguishing content types.

Established search targets include:

- articles
- wiki pages
- quizzes
- contributors
- communities
- users
- match profiles
- events

Not all of these are Phase One requirements.

The long-term search system is intended to support:

- multilingual indexing
- semantic enrichment
- structured metadata
- grouped result types
- dedicated full search
- language-aware result presentation
- cross-linked knowledge relationships

Search should remain useful even when optional AI enrichment is unavailable.

## AI Vision

AI is intended to function primarily as backend infrastructure rather than as a visible product identity.

Users should experience better search, better indexing, better translation, better recommendations, and better analysis without the platform centering AI in its branding.

Established backend AI use cases include:

- translation
- article enrichment
- wiki enrichment
- semantic indexing
- multilingual search preparation
- profile comparison
- moderation assistance
- content organization
- future recommendation signals

AI should not become authoritative for:

- verification
- trustworthiness
- moderation guilt
- theological truth
- relationship decisions
- bans
- editorial authority

Private match-profile analysis is intended to respect explicit user consent.

## Relationship and Matching Vision

The future relationship system is intended to be structured and compatibility-focused rather than swipe-based.

Established conceptual elements include:

- questionnaire-based compatibility
- quiz-based compatibility
- combined compatibility
- dealbreakers
- importance weighting
- structured match-profile sections
- radar or spider-chart dimensions
- familial archetypes
- explainable compatibility
- controlled profile visibility
- pseudonymity
- progressive disclosure
- mutual reveal
- household matching
- individual and household profiles
- optional AI-assisted comparison

The matching system is intended to protect privacy and attention, especially in a potentially male-heavy user base.

The exact scoring model is unresolved.

## Community Vision

The platform is intended to support communities and groups as first-class entities in the future.

A community may eventually include:

- contributors
- members
- administrators
- articles
- external links
- Telegram or social links
- events
- related wiki material
- announcements
- discussion spaces

The exact technical definition of a community is unresolved.

Telegram is intended to support early community coordination before native social tools are mature.

The website is intended to remain the durable archive and source of record.

## Monetization Vision

The project is intended to remain useful for free users.

Established monetization concepts include:

- free participation
- approximately $10 per month for Verified membership
- approximately $20 per month for advanced features
- approximately $50 per month for promotional or advertiser participation
- voluntary contributions
- future commerce
- possible events
- future media
- future merchandise
- future printed Marble Vault products

The exact pricing is not permanently locked and is intended to be validated against real usage.

The established intent is that payment should not improve compatibility scores or buy romantic visibility.

Premium features are intended to correspond to real added value such as verification, translation, advanced comparison, or promotional reach.

## Institutional Direction

The platform is intended to operate under an institutional brand rather than requiring the founder to become its public personality.

Official communication may eventually be issued through institutional identities such as editorial, moderation, or platform teams.

The founder’s personal identity, motivations, or personal use of future matching features are not intended to be necessary parts of the public product identity.

The platform should not falsely represent its ownership, staffing, or institutional structure.

## Long-Term Direction

The long-term direction is to build a durable, international platform that becomes increasingly valuable as more content, contributors, translations, communities, and users accumulate.

The intended growth path is:

- establish a high-quality content platform
- attract trusted contributors
- build a searchable archive
- strengthen the wiki
- expand multilingual access
- establish contributor and community identity
- build user retention through follows, search, alerts, and weekly summaries
- introduce matching only after sufficient trust and platform utility exist
- add verified membership
- add premium relationship tools
- add translated interpersonal communication
- add richer community functionality
- reinvest revenue into moderation, editorial work, translation, social media, events, media, and platform growth

The platform is intended to become more credible through consistent operation, useful content, contributor participation, and longevity.
